\begin{abstract}
This paper presents a reproducible data-processing pipeline for a planned observational study on short-form video consumption and student procrastination. As the empirical data collection has not yet begun, we evaluate the engineering readiness of the pipeline using synthetic diary data. We compare a baseline aggregation method (multiple grouped merges) with a proposed one-pass aggregation method across workloads from 10 to 10,000 rows. Results suggest that the proposed method reduces median execution time by 1.85$\times$ to 4.26$\times$, with a 0\% error rate across 800 measured runs. The pipeline is therefore validated as a fast and stable tool for future analysis of real participant data.
\end{abstract}
